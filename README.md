# DJ Buddy for Mac

A small bubble that floats over your DJ software and opens into what to play next, from **your own library**. Drag any pick onto a deck.

**[Download DJ Buddy for Mac](https://github.com/Langstonia/djbuddy-mac-releases/releases/latest/download/DJ-Buddy.dmg)**: macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel. Signed and notarized by Apple.

- **Works with Traktor, rekordbox, Serato and VirtualDJ.** Traktor live through Broadcasting; the others from their play history as you play.
- **My Style**: learns what you actually play next from your own DJ history.
- **Deep Cuts**: tracks that mix well but you rarely play.
- **AI**: DJ Buddy's engine picks the song; your library supplies your best copy of it.
- Harmonic, BPM and crate filters. It knows what's playing through Traktor Broadcasting (`localhost:8010`, Ogg Vorbis), and it passes the stream on to PULSE if you run both.
- It never takes keyboard focus from your DJ software.

DJ Buddy updates itself. New versions download in the background and install when you quit it, never mid-set.

This repository only holds the builds and the update feed. More at [djbuddy.io](https://djbuddy.io).
